Strawberry Clicker

Pixel-art idle clicker game
Godot 4 C# Windows / Linux Android Released on itch.io

About

Strawberry Clicker is an idle clicker where one big strawberry grows into a whole garden. Tap the berry, buy producers from potted plants and fields to greenhouses and factories, and give each one its own upgrades. The garden on screen fills up with everything you own. Strawberries also rot over time, so part of the game is protecting your harvest with crates, a cool cellar, a fridge and a freezer facility. Fields, factories and labs have specialization branches you can respec later. The game works fully offline, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
