Island Project

Low-poly island colony game, built in Godot + C#
Godot 4.7 C# / .NET 8 PC + Android Pre-alpha

About

A 3D low-poly colony game in the spirit of My Tribe. You start on a procedurally generated home island and grow a settlement there; the long-term plan is sailing out to other islands, settling them and trading between them. Right now it's a solid technical foundation without a gameplay loop yet: island generation, water, sky, camera and building placement all work, colonists and resources come next.

Highlights

Seeded island generation

Every island comes from a seed, so the same seed gives the same island on every platform. Shapes, hills with walkable plateaus, beaches, forest and rock zones, and props scattered with Poisson-disk sampling.

Day/night cycle

A 30-minute day with a warped sun path (longer days, quicker nights), sun and moon lights, and a custom sky shader with sunsets, stars, a cratered moon and drifting clouds.

Build system

Ghost preview, 3 m grid snapping, rotation, and placement checks for slope, sea level and obstacles before anything gets placed.

Camera

RTS-style free camera that follows the terrain height, plus a cinematic orbit camera behind the main menu.

Handmade models

Every hut, tree, palm and rock in the game is a low-poly model I built by hand in Blender.
